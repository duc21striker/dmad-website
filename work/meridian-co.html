01 — The Brief

Expert firms with no digital presence lose to firms that have one.

Nigerian law firms, accountants, and consultants regularly lose premium clients to competitors whose only advantage is a better website. The expertise is real. The digital presence isn't.

MERIDIAN & CO. demonstrates how a Nigerian professional services firm should present itself online: authority through restraint, credibility through case studies, and a clear path to a consultation. It's the difference between "worth investigating" and "worth the call."

02 — What We Built

Six ways the site builds authority.

Every section is anchored to a trust signal premium clients look for.

Practice Areas

Six clearly defined services. Each one answers "what problem do you solve?"

Case Studies

Anonymised client work with real numbers. Proof before promise.

Team Credibility

Names, roles, backgrounds. The people behind the advice, presented seriously.

Outcome-Focused Stats

Years of experience, clients served, value created. Every number chosen deliberately.

Client Testimonials

Short, specific, credible. Not "great to work with" — actual outcomes.

Consultation Booking

A structured way to book a 30-minute intro call. The conversion endpoint of the site.

04 — The Approach

Restraint as a signal of quality.

  1. 01

    Position first, design second

    Before we designed a single screen, we defined the firm's positioning: who it serves, what it solves, and why it's different. Every section on the site flows from that clarity.

  2. 02

    Build trust section by section

    Practice areas establish scope. Case studies establish proof. Team establishes credibility. Testimonials establish warmth. Each section is a trust signal in sequence.

  3. 03

    Design for the mobile CEO

    The decision-maker at a professional services firm is often reviewing on mobile between meetings. We designed the site to make that thirty-second review decisive.
