01 — The Brief

Skincare is a decision, not a purchase.

Skincare brands compete on trust. But most Nigerian skincare sites present products as a list — leaving the customer to figure out which one they actually need.

LUMIÉ demonstrates a different approach: a site that guides the customer from problem to product through routines, ingredients, and a routine finder — instead of a flat catalogue. It's the difference between a transaction and a relationship.

02 — What We Built

Six ways the site guides the buyer.

Every feature is anchored to a real question a skincare customer asks before they commit.

Product Catalogue

Filterable by skin goal — hydration, brightening, acne care, barrier repair.

Routine Finder

Customers pick a goal. The site recommends a four-step routine.

Ingredient Storytelling

Every key ingredient explained in plain language — with a reason to care.

Brand Story

Why the brand exists, what it stands for, and who it's for. Told in a voice that matches the product.

Customer Reviews

Real reviews attached to real products — visible on every product page.

Cart & Checkout

Familiar flow. Product to cart to payment without friction or doubt.

04 — The Approach

Guide the customer, don't overwhelm them.

  1. 01

    Lead with the goal, not the product

    Customers don't arrive looking for a specific serum. They arrive looking for a solution. We designed the site to start with the goal — hydration, brightening, barrier repair — and work backwards to products.

  2. 02

    Simplify the decision to one

    Skincare overwhelm is real. Every product page has one primary action, every ingredient has one clear explanation, and every routine has one starting point. Fewer options, better decisions.

  3. 03

    Make the brand feel like a ritual

    Skincare is an emotional category. We built the site around calm typography, soft photography, and generous spacing — so the experience itself becomes part of the promise.
