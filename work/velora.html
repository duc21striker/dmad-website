01 — The Brief

Selling through DMs doesn't scale.

Nigerian fashion brands routinely outgrow Instagram before they have a website. Orders get lost in DMs. Stock tracking is manual. Customers can't browse in their own time — they have to ask for what's available.

VELORA demonstrates a complete e-commerce experience built around a premium Nigerian fashion brand — from product browsing to a working checkout flow. It's the answer to "when do we get a real store?"

02 — What We Built

Six ways the site turns browsing into buying.

Every feature is anchored to a real decision a shopper makes before they add to cart.

Product Catalogue

Filterable by category, size, and availability. Photography at full scale.

Cart & Checkout UI

Standard, familiar flow. Cart, address, payment method — no surprises.

Collections

Seasonal collections presented as curated drops rather than a flat product list.

Lookbook

Editorial photography showing pieces styled on real people — not flat lays.

Wishlist

Let customers save pieces they're considering and return to buy later.

Mobile Shopping

Full experience designed for how Nigerian fashion customers actually shop — on their phones.

04 — The Approach

Built around the way people actually shop.

  1. 01

    Start with the feed

    Most fashion customers discover brands on Instagram. We designed every page so that a shopper arriving from a single post can find the rest of the collection in one or two taps.

  2. 02

    Treat products as content

    Photography is the product. We built the layout around full-bleed images and editorial spacing — not a cramped grid of thumbnails.

  3. 03

    Make checkout obvious

    Every screen has one primary action. Cart, address, payment — nothing more, nothing less. The fastest checkout is the one that doesn't make the customer think.
