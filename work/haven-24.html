01 — The Brief

Great space. No way for guests to see it.

Short-let operators compete on booking platforms that take 15–20% commission — and where their listing looks identical to every other one in the city. Meanwhile, repeat guests still have to book through the platform.

HAVEN 24 demonstrates what a short-let operator can build when they own the digital experience: a beautiful property site that reduces dependency on Airbnb, Booking.com, and endless WhatsApp back-and-forth.

02 — What We Built

Six ways the site wins the booking.

Every section is designed to answer a guest's question before they have to ask it.

Property Showcase

Each apartment shown at full scale — layout, furniture, view, and pricing per night.

Amenities Breakdown

Wi-Fi, parking, security, kitchen, AC — laid out clearly so guests filter themselves in or out.

Location & Nearby

Interactive map, neighbourhood notes, distance to key landmarks.

Booking Enquiry

Date range, guests, and a WhatsApp handoff. No payment friction — just intent.

Guest Reviews

Curated reviews from past guests, positioned right before the enquiry button.

Mobile-First

Most bookings are made on a phone at 11pm. Every interaction is designed for that moment.

04 — The Approach

Show the property, then get out of the way.

  1. 01

    Photography first

    Short-lets are sold on photography. We designed the layout around full-bleed property images and generous spacing — not a carousel that hides the space behind small thumbnails.

  2. 02

    Answer every question

    Guests want to know about parking, security, power, and Wi-Fi before they book. We put all of it on the page — so the enquiry that arrives is already warm.

  3. 03

    Make enquiry frictionless

    No payment walls, no mandatory accounts. A simple date range and a WhatsApp handoff. The fastest way to convert is to remove every step that isn't necessary.
