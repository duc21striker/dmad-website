01 — The Brief

A product that needed to look as ready as it already was.

The engineering was solid. The business model was proven with early users. But the interface was still a working prototype — functional, but not something you'd put in front of an investor or a paying customer.

We were brought in to take the product from "working MVP" to "investor-ready app" — a full design system, a clean onboarding flow, and a dashboard that felt confident the moment you opened it.

The result: 24 screens across onboarding, home, transfers, cards, transactions, and settings. Every component documented. Every interaction prototyped. Ready for handoff to the engineering team the same week.

02 — What We Delivered

Six pieces of the product.

Every screen was designed with the same three principles: clarity, confidence, and speed.

Onboarding Flow

Sign up, phone verification, KYC, and account setup — in under 3 minutes.

Home Dashboard

Balance at a glance, quick actions, recent transactions. No clutter.

Transfers

Send to bank accounts, contacts, or recent recipients. Confirmation without anxiety.

Virtual Cards

Create, freeze, and manage virtual cards for online payments — cleanly.

Transaction History

Filterable, searchable, and clear. Every transaction has a receipt.

Design System

Reusable components documented for the engineering team. No more ad-hoc styling.

04 — The Approach

Design for trust, then for speed.

  1. 01

    Map the money journey

    We started by mapping every moment a user interacts with money — from onboarding to sending to receiving. Every screen was placed against that journey before a single pixel was designed.

  2. 02

    Build the system, not the screens

    Components first. Buttons, cards, list items, modals — all documented in a system so the engineering team could build consistently without coming back to design for every new page.

  3. 03

    Prototype for the investor meeting

    A clickable prototype that demonstrates the full product experience — not just static screens. Something that could be handed to an investor or a beta user and instantly understood.
