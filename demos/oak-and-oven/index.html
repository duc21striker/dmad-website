The Experience

Three Chapters

01 The Kitchen

The Kitchen

Our open kitchen is the heart of the restaurant. Watch our chefs work the wood-fired ovens, coaxing smoke and char into every dish. It's theatre, but the kind you can taste.

02 The Bar

The Bar

A curated list of cocktails that reimagine Nigerian classics. The Zobo Negroni. The Chapman, rebuilt. Small-batch spirits and a wine list that knows its way around spice.

03 Private Dining

Private Dining

For celebrations that deserve their own room. Our private space seats up to 20, with a dedicated host and a menu tailored to the occasion. Quiet, warm, yours.

Our Story

From Lagos, with fire.

"I grew up watching my grandmother cook with fire. OAK & OVEN is my attempt to honour that — and push it forward."

Chef Adeola Balogun opened OAK & OVEN in 2019 with a simple idea: Nigerian food, cooked with the same reverence the world gives to French or Japanese cuisine. No shortcuts. No apologies. Just fire, patience, and the best ingredients Lagos has to offer.
