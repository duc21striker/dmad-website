TRACKING FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Where do I find my tracking number? +
Your tracking number is sent via SMS and email as soon as your shipment is created. It starts with "NX-" followed by a 8-digit code.
How often is tracking updated? +
Tracking status updates in real-time at every checkpoint: pickup, hub arrival, departure, out for delivery, and delivery confirmation.
What if my tracking shows no movement? +
Interstate shipments may show no movement for up to 12 hours while in transit between hubs. If it's been over 24 hours, contact support.
Can I change my delivery address after shipping? +
Yes, if the shipment hasn't been dispatched for final delivery. Contact our support team with your tracking number as soon as possible.
